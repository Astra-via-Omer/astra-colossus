SHARED ACCESS / YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR ROLE

Your role travels with your Astra account.

Admin accounts can run, create, test and evaluate engines and manage shared user access. Participant, reader and viewer accounts can run saved engines. Disabled accounts cannot enter the engine room.

Account access is verified by the server on entry and on every protected request. Results and source text are returned to you; Colossus does not retain them. Download results you want to keep.
